First release.

* Offline single-character Wubi86 drill, sampled by character frequency
* Answers hidden by default; press z to reveal every code the character has
* Wrong answers show a correction card labelled 一级/二级/三级/全码
* A character passes after N independent correct answers; optional full-code-only strict mode
* 错题集 for everything you get wrong, drivable on its own
* Light and dark themes, following the system setting
* Progress kept in a plain-text TSV you can read and back up
